This is a content mod that has the main goal of adding reference items from other franchises to Terraria.

The franchises this mod aims to reference include, but are not limited to:
The Binding of Isaac
Jujutsu Kaisen
Otherworld Legends
Hollow Knight
Dungeons and Dragons
Gwent
Necesse
Spelunky Classic
League of Legends
Risk of Rain
Deadlock
Elden Ring
Dark Souls 2
Magic Survival
Silksong



The total planned size of the mod is about ~90 items, and a few small changes and additions, like NPCs and status effects.

The current version includes:
* 105 accessories
* 19 status effects
* 7 armor set
* 5 unique weapons
* 1 unique ore
* and more...

Big thanks for contributing to: pil, sungle, z_e_n_., stormytuna, BasicallyIAmFox, Ykyki4, TacoConKvass, sprunolia and others

This mod is fully compatible with multiplayer, and was mostly tested with some other mods.
If you find any issues, provide detailed information about it on the workshop page or in the mod's discord.
